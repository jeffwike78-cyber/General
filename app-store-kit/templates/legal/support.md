## FAQ

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Open {{APP_NAME}} → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → {{APP_NAME}} → Cancel Subscription.

**How do I request a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**How do I delete my data?**
Open {{APP_NAME}} → Settings → Delete Account, or email us.
